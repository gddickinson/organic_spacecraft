GESTALT Fleet — 3D Models

Rotatable solid models of the main grown-vehicle forms, rendered live in the browser (no plug-ins). Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Toggle cutaway to slice a wedge and see the layered wall and a detailed interior — multi-level decks, habitation compartments, a vascular core, bridge and power nodes, ARCA's terraced farms and settlements, GRAVID's embryos at two growth stages, roots, and shelter pods. A live scale bar (top-left) reads true size at any zoom, and labels call out each feature.

Rendered by a small self-contained software engine — a lathe (solid-of-revolution) mesh generator + painter's-algorithm rasteriser with flat shading, no external libraries. Cutaway revolves a partial arc and caps the slice with the layered wall cross-section. Geometry is to the reference proportions; a concept study, not a build model. Green = living / photosynthetic · amber = structure, armour & docking · cyan = engineered systems · grey = rock.
