# `seedfall/world/` — map

Generated content: the sector, the Reaches, planets, markets.

Generated by `python -m seedfall.tests.maps --write` from each module's own docstring (the first sentence of it); the `maps` suite fails when a module is missing here. Why the pieces are shaped as they are is in [`../INTERFACE.md`](../INTERFACE.md) and [`../notes/`](../notes/README.md).

4 modules.

- `economy.py` — Markets.
- `galaxy.py` — Sector generation.
- `planets.py` — Planetary bodies and what a survey party finds standing on them.
- `regions.py` — Growing the Far Reaches: where the deep anchors stand, and what is behind them.
