# `seedfall/bridge/` — map

Driving a game from outside the window: protocol, server, client.

Generated by `python -m seedfall.tests.maps --write` from each module's own docstring (the first sentence of it); the `maps` suite fails when a module is missing here. Why the pieces are shaped as they are is in [`../INTERFACE.md`](../INTERFACE.md) and [`../notes/`](../notes/README.md).

8 modules.

- `__main__.py` — Start a bridge over a game, so something outside can drive it.
- `afoot.py` — A walk on a deck, driven over the bridge the way the Afoot screen drives one.
- `attached.py` — A bridge attached to the *running window*, so a caller drives what you see.
- `battle.py` — An engagement, fought over the bridge the way the window fights one.
- `checks.py` — What an argument from outside must be before a verb will act on it.
- `client.py` — Enough of a client to drive a bridge from a script or another process.
- `protocol.py` — The vocabulary: what an outside caller may ask a running game to do.
- `server.py` — A loopback JSON-lines server over the protocol.
